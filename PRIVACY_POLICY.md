# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 26, 2026

Last Call (the "App") provides cocktail recipes. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information.

## Information We Collect

Last Call does not collect, store, or transmit personal information.

## Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party services that collect information through the App.

## Data Retention and Security

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not retain or secure personal information on our servers.

## Children's Privacy

The App is intended for adults of legal drinking age in their location and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's features or information practices change. We will post the updated policy with a revised effective date.
